# 0035 - Specific Strength Goals

- Date: 2026-09-11
- Status: accepted

Strength goals may identify an exercise by its saved name/setup and require a
minimum kg weight and reps. One eligible completed working set can achieve the
goal. Warm-ups, unconfirmed work, pain-affected exercises, non-comparable
sessions, and different exercise setups do not qualify. Generic legacy kg goals
are cleared from the active goal display during normalization; new strength
goals must identify an exercise. The goal view shows current eligible best kg,
target kg, and kg remaining with a simple progress bar and no percentage as the
primary athlete-facing metric. This remains separate from the next-workout
target.
